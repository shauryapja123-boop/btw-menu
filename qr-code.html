Your menu, one scan away.

Enter the public link to your BTW menu to make a QR code customers can scan with their phone camera.

First, publish index.html on GitHub Pages. When this QR tool is opened on your published GitHub Pages site, it fills in your menu address automatically. You can also paste the menu link here yourself. The QR image is generated using QR Server, which receives the public link you enter.
